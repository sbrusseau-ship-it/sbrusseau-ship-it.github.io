High School

Stoney Creek High School Experience

Freshman
First year of high school — Was trying to get involved! I joined JV Cheer, was on the track team, and was apart of key club and psychology club!
Sophomore
Second year — focusing more on school. Was taking my first Advanced Placement class and started to care more about my grades. Made varsity cheer and was still doing track!
Junior
Third year — The start of college prep! Took the ACT many times! Joined student council and loved being involved. Joined a bunch more clubs gaining leadership positions in most. My cheer team won state finals and I made the varsity lacrosse team! Ton of new things going on and a ton of fun!.
Senior
Final year — The year of Lasts! Applying to college got tough! Many senior year traditions with my friends including senior spring break, senior sunrise and sunset, and the cookout! Had a ton of fun with my friends and had many goodbyes to give! The year before it all changed!

High School was plenty fun but change was needed to come!

I am so grateful for all the memories it gave me and all the fun we had but I am excited and love my next chapter. High School was a time of great change. It was where I learned how to study and take real tests. A place where GPA actually mattered. High School taught me that being involved leads to greater oppurtunities and to be nice to everyone! If I had not worked so hard in High School I never would have gotten into here, UW-Madison!
